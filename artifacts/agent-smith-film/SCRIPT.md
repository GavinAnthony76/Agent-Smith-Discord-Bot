# Agent Smith / Hallowed Core — 55-second film

## Director's treatment

**Promise:** A community with three identities, maintained with persistent records and human judgment.
**Format:** Approved widescreen 16:9, 55 seconds. No narration or SFX.
**Identity:** Use the supplied Hallowed Core emblem unchanged. Extended palette sampled visually: near-black #080b10, ivory #f4ead7, warm gold #e7b96a, gaming blue #64aaff, tech mint #5ee4c1. These are film colors, not a claim of an official token system. Space Grotesk display, DM Sans body, JetBrains Mono annotations.
**Hero:** The existing gold church/cross, blue game-controller shield and teal circuit/cube emblem. It is one persistent element, not duplicated in scene transitions.
**Motion:** Scale-to-focus emblem reveal, traced network/process diagrams, timed record assembly and matched geometry transitions. Rich choreography rather than a deck of headings.
**Arc:** Immediate identity, expanding community, deliberate consent and human approval, guarded reports, durable maintenance, optional configured tools, then a contrasting ivory brand finale.
**Payoff:** The noir mechanism shots resolve into a restrained large ivory lockup; the emblem returns to the opener's position for the loop.

## Asset inventory and factual boundaries

- Verified 512 × 512 supplied PNG: `attached_assets/hallowed-core-current-server-icon.png`. The source black field is preserved/blended against dark scenes; do not fabricate or recolor the emblem.
- SVG process geometry and Lucide icons are original illustrative artwork. All records use generic labels, no real identities, report text, IDs, secrets, hosting screenshots or operational counts.
- Current source confirms integrated Owner-approved feed polling, durable cases, protected targets, consent/access handling and progression. The older maintenance report predates feed and historical-review merges.
- Never say “always online,” “24/7,” “fully secure,” “exactly once,” or “AI decides.” No staged-default or optional-feature-live claim.
- One generated 55-second instrumental bed: cinematic electronic, restrained low percussion, warm harmonic lift and resolved close. No voiceover/SFX. Audio-to-scene seek alignment will be checked; individual beats are not claimed synchronized to musical downbeats.

## Implementation reconciliation

The built diagrams are intentionally illustrative rather than replicas of live Discord UI. The persistent official emblem remains as a small upper-right signature in moderation, maintenance and tools as well; it is not hidden or duplicated. Across boundaries it combines position/size reframing with overlapping scene scale/opacity, while each shot's last 17% uses a slight perspective pull and continued process geometry. Database ellipses and report rows are complementary gold geometry rather than a literal topology morph. The human review ring pulses/traces and changes from person to check, rather than using a rotating stamp. The opening's escalating masked type, tightening tracking and expanding orbit replace the planned macro crop. Closing copy fades from 4.85–5.30 before the emblem traverses its reading region; the readable ivory lockup holds for more than two seconds. Audio generation returned a longer file than requested, so the single bed was trimmed to 55 seconds with a one-second tail fade, not regenerated. Canonical offsets remain 0 / 5 / 12 / 20 / 28 / 35 / 42 / 49 seconds. Initial buffering correction seeks to the elapsed scene time, while pause/resume preserves the frozen audio position.
